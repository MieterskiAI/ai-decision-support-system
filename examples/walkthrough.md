# Walkthrough (Input → Elimination → Output)

This walkthrough shows how a minimal input could be translated into signals and a decision report.
It is illustrative and keeps the human in the loop.

## 1) Input summary

Source: [`sample_input.json`](sample_input.json)

- **Profile:** non-technical, 9-month timeline, limited weekly hours.
- **Work style:** prefers structure, likes explaining, low ambiguity tolerance.
- **Tolerance:** medium debugging tolerance, low ambiguity tolerance.
- **Expectations:** 6 hours/week, wants a first paid pilot.

## 2) Signals derived (qualitative)

- Structure-seeking → favors operational / process-heavy paths.
- Low ambiguity tolerance → avoid paths that demand open-ended experimentation.
- Medium debugging tolerance + limited time → avoid heavy engineering ramps.
- Likes explaining → consulting/implementation could fit.

## 3) Path elimination steps

1. **Prompt Engineering / Interaction Design**
   - Hard stop due to low ambiguity tolerance and dislike of exploration.
2. **AI Engineering (Light)**
   - Risk flag due to limited weekly hours and moderate debugging tolerance.
3. **AI Automation / AI Operations**
   - Support signal due to structure preference and operational mindset.
4. **AI Consulting / Implementation**
   - Support signal due to preference for explaining and structured delivery.

## 4) Outcome (summary)

- **Primary recommendation:** AI Automation / AI Operations.
- **Alternative:** AI Consulting / Implementation (with domain pairing).
- **Eliminated:** Prompt Engineering (hard stop).
- **Conditional:** AI Engineering (light) only after more time is available.

## 5) Human-in-the-loop reminder

This walkthrough is a guide. A human reviewer should confirm fit and adjust based on context.
