# KPIs (PLANNED)

No measured KPI outcomes are currently stored in this repository.
All metrics below are **PLANNED** and should be treated as placeholders for future implementation.

## KPI 1: Recommendation Clarity Rate (PLANNED)
- **Definition:** % of reports where user and reviewer agree the recommendation is understandable.
- **How to measure:** post-report survey (Yes/No or 1–5 scale converted to pass threshold).
- **What good looks like (PLANNED target):** >= 85% clarity.

## KPI 2: Rework Rate (PLANNED)
- **Definition:** % of cases requiring full re-run due to missing/ambiguous intake data.
- **How to measure:** count cases tagged `iteration-required` before first usable recommendation.
- **What good looks like (PLANNED target):** <= 25% after intake form refinement.

## KPI 3: Decision Confidence Coverage (PLANNED)
- **Definition:** % of reports that include explicit rationale for recommendation and elimination.
- **How to measure:** checklist-based QA on report sections.
- **What good looks like (PLANNED target):** 100% of reports include rationale blocks.

## KPI 4: Human Override Frequency (PLANNED)
- **Definition:** % of draft recommendations changed at human review step.
- **How to measure:** compare draft vs approved recommendation labels.
- **What good looks like (PLANNED target):** 10-30% (healthy oversight, not blind acceptance).

## KPI 5: Time-to-Decision (PLANNED)
- **Definition:** median elapsed time from completed intake to approved decision report.
- **How to measure:** timestamp intake completion and review approval.
- **What good looks like (PLANNED target):** <= 2 business days for standard cases.
